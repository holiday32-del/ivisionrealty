Buyer representation

A clearer path from search to keys.

1. Define the goal

Clarify location, property type, timing, priorities and budget before narrowing the search.

2. Search strategically

Explore available properties and identify homes that fit both your practical needs and longer-term plans.

3. Tour and evaluate

Coordinate showings and evaluate condition, location, comparable properties and potential tradeoffs.

4. Build the offer

Structure price and terms with attention to competition, contingencies, timing and your overall position.

5. Navigate due diligence

Coordinate inspections, disclosures, appraisal, financing milestones and contractual deadlines.

6. Reach closing

Stay informed through final documentation, settlement and possession of the property.
