A focused selling process

From market preparation to closing.

1. Understand the market

Review recent comparable sales, active competition, property condition and local buyer demand to establish a practical pricing strategy.

2. Prepare and position

Identify presentation priorities, showing readiness and improvements that may help the property compete effectively.

3. Market the listing

Launch with clear positioning, buyer-facing marketing and coordinated exposure designed to attract qualified interest.

4. Evaluate offers

Compare price, financing, contingencies, timing and other terms instead of focusing only on the headline number.

5. Negotiate and manage

Coordinate inspections, appraisal, disclosures, lender milestones and contractual deadlines through the transaction.

6. Close with clarity

Stay informed through final documentation, settlement and possession so there are fewer last-minute surprises.
