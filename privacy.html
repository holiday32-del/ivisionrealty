iVision Realty collects information you choose to provide through website forms, including your name, email address, phone number, financing or real-estate interest, and any message you submit. Information may be shared with service providers and business systems used to respond to inquiries, including customer relationship management platforms, and with lenders, real-estate professionals, settlement providers, or other parties when necessary to provide services you request or when required by law. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Information is retained for business, legal, compliance, and recordkeeping purposes as appropriate.
